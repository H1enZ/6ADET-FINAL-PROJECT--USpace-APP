Getting to know USpace, a little better.

What is USpace?

USpace is a web app for two people in a relationship. It brings your conversations, memories, love notes, moods, and plans into one shared space. It works in your browser with an internet connection.

How do I connect with my partner?

Create your account and choose “Start our space.” You’ll receive a six-character invite code. Your partner creates their own account, enters that code, and chooses “Join space.” Each space supports exactly two people.

Can we save our memories together?

Yes. Add a story, a location, tags, and up to ten photos to a memory. Your shared Timeline turns them into a scrapbook you can arrange and revisit together. Love Notes and Time Capsules give your words a place, too.

Is our information private?

The app uses signed-in accounts, database rules that restrict shared records to the couple, and private photo storage. It does not offer end-to-end encryption. Therabot processes conversations through an AI service. This is an evolving project with documented security limitations; review the security and privacy notes (opens in a new tab) before adding sensitive information.

What features are available?

Explore shared messaging, the photo Timeline, Love Notes, Time Capsules, daily mood sharing and questions, anniversary and special-day countdowns, a Bucket List with savings tracking, and Therabot’s private and couple reflection modes.

How can we start using USpace?

Open the USpace web app (opens in a new tab), continue past the welcome screen, and choose “Create an account.” There’s no app-store download to install. The app needs an internet connection. If the demo is temporarily unavailable, try again later.
